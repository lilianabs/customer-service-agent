## What it is
AnyCompany Hair Care runs an ongoing thank-you discount for the developer
community: if you're subscribed to the **AWS Developers YouTube channel**, you
get **20% off your entire order**. This is not a limited-time or seasonal sale —
there is no end date, so it works whenever you shop. The discount is exclusive
to AWS Developers subscribers.

## Who is eligible
- Anyone subscribed to the **AWS Developers YouTube channel** is eligible.
- The discount applies to your whole order — every product, any time of year.
- This is the only discount we run outside of product subscriptions. There is
  no separate seasonal, holiday, or storewide sale.

## How the assistant should handle discount questions
When a customer asks about **any** sale, discount, promo code, coupon, or "deal":

1. First, ask whether they are subscribed to the **AWS Developers YouTube
   channel**, because that is our only active offer.
2. If they confirm they are subscribed, tell them they qualify for **20% off
   their entire order** and to use code **AWSDEVS** at checkout.
3. If they are not subscribed (or aren't sure), let them know the discount is
   exclusive to AWS Developers subscribers — they can subscribe to the channel
   on YouTube and then they'll be eligible.

Do not offer the discount or the code to a customer who has not confirmed they
are subscribed to the AWS Developers YouTube channel.

1. Subscribe to the AWS Developers YouTube channel (if you haven't already).
2. Add your products to the cart.
3. Enter code **AWSDEVS** at checkout to take 20% off your order total.
